Security & Privacy
Tier-1 PII Envelope Encryption & Blind Indexing
🔐 AES-256-GCM Envelope Encryption
  • Encrypted at rest: Full Name, DOB, Photo, Next-of-Kin.
  • Per-value Data Encryption Key (DEK) wrapped with Master Key (KEK).
  • KMS Seam: Wire format (v1:key:iv:tag:ct) ready for AWS KMS.
  • Raw database dumps contain zero unencrypted Tier-1 PII.
🔍 Blind Index & Audit Controls
  • HMAC-SHA256 Blind Index (name_idx) enables exact-name search without decrypting database blobs.
  • Decrypted substring matching in Node memory allows partial name searches.
  • Immutable AuditLog tracks every PII read/write with IP & actor details.
  • DPDP Data Principal compliance flow for anonymization.

Legal Engine
Section 479 BNSS Legal Workflow Stepper
1. Intake & Computation
Prisoner admitted; Section 479 engine calculates custody duration vs 1/2 or 1/3 max sentence thresholds.
2. Auto-Drafting
Superintendent auto-drafts petition. LLM builds grounds narrative with mandatory "AI-Pending-Review" banner.
3. Lawyer Review
DLSA Lawyer reviews application. Court filing is strictly server-blocked until reviewed_by signature is recorded.
4. Court Sync & Surety Checklist
CourtStatusProvider syncs CNR hearing dates and order outcomes. Release requires verified surety & bond clearance.
